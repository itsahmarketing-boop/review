Privacy Policy

AH Marketing (Talertek, Khilkhet, Dhaka, Bangladesh) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the AH Marketing social media tool ("the app") handles information. The app is an internal tool used by AH Marketing to plan, review and publish posts on its own Facebook Pages, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube accounts, and to send WhatsApp notifications to its own team.

Information we process

  • Content we create for our own pages (text, images, videos).
  • Page and account identifiers and access tokens for accounts we manage, stored securely in Google Apps Script properties.
  • For WhatsApp: the phone number and time of messages sent to our business number, used only to know whether a free reply window is open. Message content is not stored.

How we use it

Only to publish our own content and to notify our team. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention

WhatsApp phone/time records are deleted automatically after 3 days. Other data is kept only as long as needed to run our pages.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any information, send a message to the AH Marketing Facebook Page (facebook.com/itisahmarketing). We will delete it within 7 days.
